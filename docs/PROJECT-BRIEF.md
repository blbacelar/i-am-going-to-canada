# Project brief

## Project

Full revamp of [iamgoingtocanada.ca](https://iamgoingtocanada.ca/) for a Canadian immigration consulting company led by Marina Snyder and supported by multiple consultants.

## Business objective

Turn the website into a multilingual presentation and booking funnel for four consultants. The company should feel larger than one personal brand while retaining Marina's credibility as founder/owner.

## Primary conversion

Help a visitor understand the company, find the most relevant professional and book an appointment through that consultant's Calendly link.

- Virginia Melo

All biographies, credentials, languages, services, licence details, photos and Calendly links must be verified. Never infer missing facts.

## Audiences

- People outside Canada researching lawful immigration pathways.
- People already in Canada who need professional guidance.
- Portuguese-, English- and French-speaking individuals, couples and families.
- Visitors at different stages: exploration, planning, document preparation and case-specific consultation.

## Audience state

Visitors often arrive with:

- too much conflicting information;
- fear of making an expensive decision;
- uncertainty about which service or professional they need;
- concern about trust, regulation and professional competence;
- desire for clarity without pressure or unrealistic promises.

## Brand promise

Provide a clear, professional and human route to the right conversation. The website should reduce confusion and help visitors take an appropriate next step without pretending to determine their eligibility.

## Recommended concept

### The Right Conversation

The website behaves like a multilingual digital concierge. Instead of forcing visitors to read every service page, it helps them identify the kind of support they need and discover the consultants who offer it.

Suggested thematic copy, subject to content review:

- EN: “Your Canadian journey starts with the right conversation.”
- FR: “Votre projet canadien commence par la bonne conversation.”
- PT: “Seu projeto no Canadá começa com a conversa certa.”

These lines are provisional marketing copy, not approved factual content.

## Desired emotional qualities

- Trustworthy
- Clear
- Welcoming
- Modern
- International
- Calm
- Competent
- Personal without being personality-dependent

## Site goals

1. Establish the business and team within the first screen.
2. Explain services in plain language.
3. Show all four professionals fairly.
4. Let visitors compare expertise, languages and availability.
5. Reduce friction between arrival and booking.
6. Build confidence through verified credentials, process clarity and real testimonials.
7. Support discoverability in English, French and Portuguese.

## Suggested information architecture

- Home
- Find your consultant / concierge
- Consultants
  - One localized profile route per consultant
- Services
  - Localized service detail routes where content supports them
- How it works
- About the company
- Resources or articles, only if existing content can be migrated responsibly
- FAQ
- Contact / booking
- Privacy policy
- Terms and immigration-information disclaimer

## Homepage narrative

1. Global navigation and immediate language choice.
2. Hero focused on finding clear, professional guidance.
3. Trust strip with verified facts only.
4. Short “What do you need help with?” concierge entry.
5. Four-consultant team presentation.
6. Services and who each is for.
7. Simple process: explore, meet, plan.
8. Verified testimonials or outcomes, without promises.
9. FAQ addressing booking and service-selection friction.
10. Final CTA to find a consultant or view the full team.

## Concierge behavior

The flow may ask non-sensitive navigation questions such as:

- Which language do you prefer?
- Are you exploring options, already preparing a process or seeking help with an existing matter?
- Which broad service category are you looking for?
- Would you like to choose a professional or see suggested matches?

It must not calculate points, assess eligibility, predict approval, recommend a legal pathway or store personal immigration facts.

## Scope for version one

Included:

- complete responsive redesign;
- EN/FR/PT routing and content structure;
- consultant directory driven by JSON;
- concierge/matching interface;
- Calendly handoff;
- basic SEO, structured data and analytics events;
- legal/disclaimer pages based on verified existing content;
- placeholder photography system;
- local and preview deployment readiness.

Not included unless later approved:

- database;
- CMS or admin dashboard;
- authentication;
- payments;
- immigration eligibility engine;
- automated legal advice;
- storage of case details;
- replacement of Calendly with custom scheduling.

## Success measures

Instrument these events without collecting sensitive profile data:

- language selected;
- concierge started;
- service category selected;
- consultant result viewed;
- consultant profile viewed;
- Calendly CTA clicked;
- contact action clicked;

Primary business KPI: qualified Calendly clicks and completed bookings, if Calendly analytics can be measured compliantly.
