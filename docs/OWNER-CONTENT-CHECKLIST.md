## Photography brief

For a consistent four-person team presentation, request:

- vertical high-resolution portrait;
- landscape or wider crop when available;
- similar lighting and colour temperature;
- simple complementary background;
- natural expression and direct/near-direct eye line;
- wardrobe appropriate to a premium professional service;
- permission for website, advertising and social use;
- original files without screenshots or social-media compression.
