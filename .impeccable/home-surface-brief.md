# Home surface brief

- Scope: localized home routes `/en`, `/fr` and `/pt`; visitor mode `Persuade`.
- Audience: people seeking clear professional Canadian immigration support while uncertain about service and consultant choice.
- Job: understand the company and team, identify a broad support need, compare appropriate professionals and continue to a verified booking destination.
- Primary action: start the consultant finder. Secondary action: meet the full team.
- Proof: four visible professionals, public language/service attributes, current-site published regulated facts only when marked for owner confirmation, transparent process and explicit advice boundary.
- Constraints: equal consultant prominence, complete EN/FR/PT, no eligibility assessment, no invented claims, no realistic substitute faces, resilient French layout.
- Chosen structure: the concierge is the compositional centre. A thesis hero establishes the team, a four-field route mosaic proves it visually, and the next major surface is the guided choice itself; later sections alternate team, services, process and quiet disclosures.
- Memorable moment: one route line moves through four equal portrait fields and resolves at the concierge question, turning “the right conversation” into a visible, navigable relationship.
- Unresolved: official portraits, Aline's complete record, approved testimonials, final legal wording and owner-approved logo.
